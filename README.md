# healthcare-ai-chatbot-risks-
Healthcare AI chatbot risks beyond hallucinations. Free workflow assessment templates, governance checklists, and real-world examples for safe clinical AI deployment.
The riskiest AI chatbot in a hospital may not be the one IT approved—it's the one staff use because the sanctioned tool adds friction. This repository explores the four critical operational risks of healthcare AI chatbots: (1) answering when it should abstain, (2) not knowing who is asking, (3) being used outside approved workflows, and (4) unclear ownership of outcomes.

Inside you'll find:

Clinical AI Workflow Assessment Template - Map deployments from prompt to escalation

Healthcare AI Governance Checklist - Ensure safety, privacy, and compliance

Real-world workflow examples - See the difference between risky and governed patterns

Built for healthcare IT leaders, clinical governance teams, and AI safety officers who need practical tools—not just theory.
